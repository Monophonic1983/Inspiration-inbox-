# Privacy policy: Inspiration Inbox

_Draft for legal review before submission. Replace the contact line._

**What the app stores.** Your thoughts (the words you spoke or typed) and, if you allow it, the audio of what you said. They are stored on your iPhone. If you use iCloud, your thoughts are also synced through your own private iCloud database; we cannot see it. Audio is never synced.

**Audio.** Audio is recorded only while you capture a thought, stays on your device, and is deleted after the period you choose in Settings (30 days by default; "Don't keep" and Private mode keep none).

**Speech and AI.** Speech is converted to text on your device. By default your thoughts are structured by an AI model that runs on your device. Nothing leaves your phone.

**Enhanced mode (optional, off by default).** If you turn it on after reading the consent screen, the text of each thought is sent through our server to Anthropic to be structured, then returned. Audio is never sent. Our server does not store or log your text. Turn it off any time in Settings; Private mode disables it.

**What we don't do.** No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no sale of data.

**Your control.** Delete any thought in the app; delete downloaded AI models in Settings; deleting the app removes local data.

**Contact.** _your email_
