# App Store listing: English

**Name:** Inspiration Inbox
**Subtitle:** Speak a thought. Get a clean note.

**Promotional text:** One press, one spoken thought, and a tidy note is waiting before you put your phone down.

**Description**
Inspiration Inbox is the fastest way to catch an idea. Press the Action Button, say what's on your mind, and it becomes a clean, structured note: a title, tidy text, a type, tags and action items.

• One press to record, straight from the Action Button, Control Center or Lock Screen
• Live transcript in English and Chinese, even offline
• AI that edits, never invents: it cleans up what you said and adds nothing
• Your original words are always saved first, one tap away
• Private by default: speech and AI run on your iPhone. Cloud is optional and asks first
• Pin, archive, search and share as Markdown or text
• Syncs through your private iCloud

No folders. No setup. Just capture.
