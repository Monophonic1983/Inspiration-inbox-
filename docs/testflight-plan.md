# TestFlight external beta (Phase 5)

1. Archive with a production bundle id; confirm `PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy`, entitlements (App Attest environment → production) and the model catalog are final.
2. Internal test first (you, two devices) for sync and Enhanced mode.
3. External group of 10–25 people who speak English, Mandarin, and mixed. Beta review notes = `docs/app-review-notes.md`.
4. Ask each tester to use the Action Button daily for seven days and report: missed captures (must be 0), wrong-language output, slow starts, model download problems.
5. Keep a fix list in `docs/progress.md`. Gate F needs it empty after seven days.
6. Submit for App Review; fix and resubmit until approved.
